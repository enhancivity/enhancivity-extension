How Enhancivity works

  1. Wake Enhancivity on the tab where you are working.
  2. Prepare — your request plus the right project memory becomes a ready-to-paste prompt.
  3. Review & insert — you approve every word and submit in the tool yourself.

Enhancivity only reads a page when you click Wake, Prepare, or Capture — never in the background — and it never submits anything for you.
